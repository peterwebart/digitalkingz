What does the modern ecommerce customer journey look like?
Map discovery, evaluation, purchase, fulfilment, support, retention, and advocacy across search, social, marketplaces,
reviews, email, and referrals.

What are the most common ecommerce marketing mistakes?
Address traffic-first thinking, weak feed quality, paid traffic sent to poor product pages, siloed teams, discount
dependency, poor delivery information, duplicate manufacturer copy, and uncontrolled automation.

How should you create an ecommerce marketing strategy?
How do you set revenue goals and ecommerce KPIs?
Start with revenue, gross margin, stock availability, growth targets, constraints, and an ownership model across
marketing, data, merchandising, web, inventory, CRM, and customer experience.

How do you define your target customer and buying journey?
Segment by use case, value, frequency, industry, geography, decision-maker role, and awareness stage; identify
objections such as compatibility, delivery, price, payment terms, returns, and approval.

How do you select ecommerce marketing channels?
Compare ecommerce SEO, Google Shopping, paid search, paid social, email and SMS, content, marketplaces, and
partnerships by commercial role, readiness, margin, and measurement confidence.

How do you create a channel-prioritization model?
Score channels by demand, CAC, time to value, tracking confidence, contribution margin, operational readiness, and
scalability. Separate demand capture from demand creation.

How should B2B ecommerce marketing differ?
Include technical specifications, compatibility, documentation, certifications, volume pricing, quote workflows,
negotiated accounts, reordering, and procurement-friendly payment options.

How do you optimize product discovery and conversion?
How do ecommerce SEO and category architecture improve product discovery?
Build crawlable category, subcategory, product, brand, use-case, and resource pages. Control faceted navigation,
duplicate filter URLs, titles, headings, internal links, and technical specifications.

How do you optimize product pages for buyers and search engines?
Use accurate titles, images, pricing, availability, variants, specification tables, delivery, returns, reviews, FAQs, related
products, product documentation, and direct calls to action.

How should teams manage Google Merchant Center and product feeds?
Treat feed data as commercial infrastructure. Maintain accurate product IDs, titles, descriptions, URLs, images, prices,
availability, GTINs, shipping, returns, category data, and synchronization with inventory systems.

How should Product and Offer schema be implemented?
Use accurate markup only on eligible pages and ensure price, availability, shipping, returns, identifiers, imagery, and
variants match the visible page and feed data. Validate before scaled deployment.

How do you reduce cart and checkout abandonment?

Show delivery information early, minimize fields, offer suitable payment methods, remove mobile friction, enable guest

How do you build trust and EEAT into ecommerce pages?
Publish accurate business, contact, return, warranty, privacy, product, price, availability, and support information. Use

How do you acquire and retain ecommerce customers?
How should businesses use paid search and Google Shopping?
Separate brand, non-brand, shopping, competitor, and remarketing activity. Align bids and budgets to margin,
inventory, seasonality, product performance, landing-page relevance, and tracking.

How should businesses use paid social media?
Use visual creative for discovery, audience development, creative testing, retargeting, and expansion. Measure with
business outcomes—not clicks alone.

How should email, SMS, and CRM support retention?
Cover welcome, browse abandonment, cart abandonment, post-purchase education, delivery, support, replenishment,
review, cross-sell, win-back, loyalty, VIP, and B2B reorder flows.

How do personalization and marketing automation improve ecommerce results?
Segment by behavior, product interest, purchase history, lifecycle, stock, and consent. Use frequency caps,
suppression rules, quality controls, and regular audits.

How do content, creators, reviews, and partnerships support demand generation?
Develop buying guides, comparisons, use-case content, tutorials, technical explainers, customer stories, qualified
creator relationships, affiliates, distributors, and transparent commercial disclosures.

How do you measure, govern, and scale ecommerce marketing?
Which ecommerce marketing metrics belong on an executive dashboard?
Include acquisition, conversion, revenue, gross profit, AOV, retention, CLV, feed health, product availability, return rate,
page experience, and customer-support metrics.

How do you build reliable ecommerce attribution?
Use consistent naming, UTMs, consent-aware analytics, storefront and CRM integration, finance comparison, cohort
analysis, and incrementality testing where mature enough.

How do you improve marketing efficiency without damaging growth?
Segment products by margin, stock, conversion, returns, and lifetime value; reduce persistently unprofitable spend;
improve conversion before scaling spend; use controlled testing.

How should teams govern ecommerce marketing data and technology?
Define sources of truth for product, price, stock, customer, order, and campaign data. Assign owners, document
integrations, maintain access control, audit data, and plan incident response.

Should you build, buy, or outsource ecommerce marketing?
Compare in-house, agency, consultant, software, and hybrid models based on expertise, speed, catalog complexity,
data maturity, internal capacity, and ownership needs.
